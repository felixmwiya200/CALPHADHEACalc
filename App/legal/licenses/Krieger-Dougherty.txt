KRIEGER-DOUGHERTY SEMI-SOLID SUSPENSION RHEOLOGY MODEL

Bibliographic Reference:
Irvine M. Krieger and Thomas J. Dougherty,
"A Mechanism for Non-Newtonian Flow in Suspensions of Rigid Spheres",
Transactions of the Society of Rheology, Vol. 3, No. 1, pp. 137-152 (1959).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1122/1.548848

Implementation Notice:
The semi-solid slurry apparent dynamic viscosity across the solidus-liquidus
mushy zone (mu_eff(f_s) = mu_L * [1 - f_s / phi_m]^(-2.5 * phi_m)) implemented in
CALPHADHEACalc is based on the classical Krieger-Dougherty constitutive model for
concentrated particulate suspensions. Formulas represent scientific principles in
the public domain.
