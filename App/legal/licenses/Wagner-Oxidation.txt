Bibliographic Reference:
Carl Wagner,
"Theoretical Analysis of the Diffusion Processes Determining the Oxidation Rate of Alloys",
Journal of the Electrochemical Society, Vol. 99, No. 10, pp. 369-380 (1952).

Implementation Notice:
The high-temperature oxidation kinetics, parabolic rate constants (k_p(T)),
and Wagner critical solute concentrations (N_Cr*, N_Al*) for selective protective
scale formation are implemented in CALPHADHEACalc based on classical diffusion-
controlled oxidation theory. The equations represent fundamental physics in the
public domain.
