Bibliographic Reference:
C. Varvenne, A. Luque, and W.A. Curtin,
"Theory of strengthening in concentrated alloys",
Acta Materialia, Vol. 118, pp. 164-176 (2016).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actamat.2016.07.040

Implementation Notice:
The temperature-dependent yield strength formulations and solute volume misfit
strengthening equations implemented in CALPHADHEACalc's mechanical engine are
derived from the dislocation-solute interaction theory developed by Varvenne, Luque,
and Curtin (2016). Formulas represent published scientific principles used for
computational alloy design and educational prediction.
